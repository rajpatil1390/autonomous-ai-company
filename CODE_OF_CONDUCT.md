# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for every
participant, regardless of background, identity, experience, or role.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate with empathy, patience, and technical honesty.
- Critique ideas and changes without attacking people.
- Respect privacy, consent, attribution, and responsible disclosure.
- Make space for different levels of experience and ask clarifying questions.
- Accept correction, repair harm, and prioritize community safety.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, sustained disruption, and publishing private information
without permission are unacceptable in project spaces or when representing the
project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainers or the
private process described in [SECURITY_CONTACT.md](SECURITY_CONTACT.md) when a
security or privacy issue is involved. Do not open a public issue containing
personal or sensitive details.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, limit participation, issue a
warning, impose a temporary ban, or permanently remove a participant. Actions
will be proportionate to impact, pattern, context, and community safety.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository discussions, reviews, project events, and
public representation of the project. Good-faith reports will be handled as
confidentially as practical, and retaliation is prohibited.
